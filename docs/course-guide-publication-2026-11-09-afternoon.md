# Course guide publication for November 9, afternoon

Three public-course guides were published for the first time. Each passed draft validation, a fresh read of its sources, and production database readback. The publication filled verified locality fields for Forest Park and Durand Eastman. Readback found no changes to provider execution fields. Physical layout details appear in the guide text.

- [Forest Park Golf Course](https://teetimespot.com/courses/forest-park-golf-course-woodhaven-ny) in Woodhaven, New York, has 18 public holes, elevated greens, and a clubhouse in a Queens city park. The city's concession plan matches the operator's Woodhaven location. [GolfNYC course guide](https://www.golfnyc.com/forest-park-course/), [GolfNYC scorecard](https://www.golfnyc.com/forest-park-scorecard/), [NYC concession plan](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/mocs/downloads/Opportunities/FCRC/plans/AgencyAnnualConcessionPlanCitywide_FY2027.pdf).
- [Durand Eastman Golf Course](https://teetimespot.com/courses/durand-eastman-golf-course-rochester-ny) in Rochester, New York, has 18 public holes in Durand Eastman Park. The guide describes the Robert Trent Jones Sr. routing, rolling fairways, creeks, and ponds. The county and operator list the same street address. [Monroe County Parks](https://www.monroecounty.gov/parks-golf), [course overview](https://monroecountyparksgolf.com/durand-eastman/).
- [The Neuse Country Club](https://teetimespot.com/courses/the-neuse-country-club-clayton-nc) in Clayton, North Carolina, offers public reservations on its 18-hole course. The guide describes the uphill fifth, downhill doglegs, and practice and clubhouse facilities. [Club golf page](https://neusegolf.com/golf/), [hole-by-hole guide](https://neusegolf.com/golf/course-information/).
